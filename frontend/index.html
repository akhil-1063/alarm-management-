How the system works

Step 1

Connect hardware

Enable your phone hotspot, connect the laptop and the ESP32 to it, and configure the laptop's hotspot IPv4 address in the firmware config.

Step 2

Register & assign

Administrators register devices and issue doctor accounts; each device is assigned to exactly one patient so readings can never cross records.

Step 3

Stream readings

The ESP32 POSTs validated JSON to /api/v1/ingest. The backend authenticates the device, timestamps and stores the data.

Step 4

Warn & respond

The risk engine raises explainable alerts, dashboards update live, and clinicians acknowledge, annotate and resolve with a complete event history.
