Privacy & Medical-Safety Information

ICU Sentinel is a research and educational prototype. It is not a clinically validated diagnostic or life-support system, and it is not certified as a medical device under any regulation (EU MDR, FDA, CDSCO or otherwise).

Medical safety

  • Not a diagnosis. Heart rate and SpO₂ estimates alone cannot diagnose cardiac arrest, heart attack, stroke, sepsis or any other condition.
  • Not emergency equipment. In-application alerts do not replace hospital alarm systems, telemetry, pulse oximeters with clinical certification, or supervised monitoring.
  • Ambient ≠ body temperature. The DHT11 measures room air. It is never presented as patient body temperature and must not be used as a fever measurement.
  • SpO₂ is an estimate. MAX30102-derived SpO₂ is affected by motion, perfusion and sensor fit. Values must be confirmed with clinical-grade equipment.
  • Risk categories are software priorities produced by an explainable rule engine (thresholds, persistence, hysteresis). They are not clinically validated classifications, not a trained machine-learning model, and a “Low” priority is never proof that a patient is safe.
  • Stroke warnings arise only from structured clinician symptom entry. The sensors cannot detect stroke and cannot confirm or rule it out.

Privacy & data protection

  • All data is stored locally in an SQLite database on the machine running the backend. No cloud services are required or used for core functionality.
  • Passwords are stored only as salted hashes (PBKDF2-SHA256, 200k iterations). Device credentials are unique per device and never returned in ordinary dashboard responses or logged.
  • Access is enforced server-side: patients can only read their own records, doctors only assigned patients, and every access-relevant action is written to an audit log.
  • This local deployment is not automatically compliant with HIPAA, GDPR, DPDP Act or other healthcare-privacy requirements. Do not load real patient data without appropriate authorisation, consent, access controls and institutional approval.
  • Cookies used for sessions are HttpOnly, SameSite=Lax, and expire with the configured session window.

Simulated test data

The developer test panel generates clearly labelled simulated readings (is_simulated=True). They appear with a purple “SIMULATED” badge, are excluded from real-measurement statistics by default, are separable in CSV exports, and can be deleted without touching real sensor data. Test data is never silently mixed into real patient history.
